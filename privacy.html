Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short

Streaming Sync runs entirely in your browser. There is no Streaming Sync server: your sign-ins and your library, watch history, Continue Watching and add-ons go straight from your browser to Stremio, Nuvio and Trakt, and to no one else. Nothing is collected, tracked or sold.

What the page handles

  • Sign-ins. Passwords you type go only to the app they belong to (Stremio or Nuvio) and are never stored. Trakt sign-in happens on Trakt's own site. The page keeps only the session tokens the apps return.
  • Your data in those apps. Library, watch history, Continue Watching progress and add-ons are read from one account and written to the other, as you choose. They are held in the page's memory while it's open and are not stored.

What stays in your browser

  • With Remember accounts on this device ticked (the default), the session tokens and which accounts you picked are kept in your browser's local storage, so you stay signed in next time. Untick it, or use Sign out, to remove them. With it unticked, only that choice is kept.
  • If you enter your own Trakt app's client ID (and secret, for older apps), it is kept in local storage too.

None of this leaves your browser, except to the app it belongs to.

Who the page talks to

  • Stremio (api.strem.io, link.stremio.com), for Stremio accounts.
  • Nuvio (api.nuvio.tv), for Nuvio accounts.
  • Trakt (api.trakt.tv, auth.trakt.tv), for Trakt accounts.
  • Cinemeta (v3-cinemeta.strem.io) and MetaHub (images.metahub.space), for episode lists, titles and posters. These requests carry public title ids only, never your account.
  • Add-on hosts: when an add-on is copied, its manifest is loaded from the add-on's own address.

Each of these handles your data under its own privacy policy. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log visits (see GitHub's privacy statement). Streaming Sync has no analytics, cookies or trackers.

Your control

Sign out of an account on the page to forget it (and end the session where the page created it), or clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything. You can also revoke Streaming Sync's access from your Trakt account settings at any time.
